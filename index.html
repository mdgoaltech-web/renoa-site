Renoa Support

Renoa is a renovation planner: rooms, measurements, references, materials, contractor quotes, budget, payments, tasks, progress photos, defects and contractor instruction packs, all in one project on your device.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? Only on your device. Renoa needs no account and uploads nothing.

Do I need Renoa Plus? No. The free version has one active project with up to three rooms. Plus adds more projects and rooms, more storage, instruction-pack issuing and advanced comparison and reports. Viewing and editing what you already entered is never blocked.

How do I restore a purchase? Open More → Subscription and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel? iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. The 90-day Renovation Pass does not renew.

Are the measurements and quantities exact? They are planning estimates. Measurements marked Estimate are not suitable for cutting or ordering; confirm with your contractor or supplier.

Will I lose my project if I delete the app? Yes. Everything lives on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. Back up your device to keep it safe. Instruction packs and reports can be exported as PDF from the app.

Which languages are supported? English and Urdu. Instruction packs can be issued in either language regardless of the app language.
