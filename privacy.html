Renoa Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Renoa ("the app") is a renovation planner made by AFEX Digital. It is designed to keep your information on your device. Contact: faiza.zulfiqar71@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect nothing about you. Renoa needs no account. We do not run servers that receive your data, we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter — projects, rooms, measurements, reference images, materials, contractors, quotes, budgets, expenses, payments, change requests, tasks, photos, defects, documents and instruction packs — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Photos. When you import or take a photo, Renoa re-saves it without camera metadata, including GPS location. Camera and photo-library access is requested only when you choose to add a photo and is used for nothing else.

Assistant drafts. The assistant drafts tasks, quote lines, summaries and instructions on your device. No text is sent to any server.

Sharing. Sharing a PDF, CSV, file or contact is always started by you through your device's share sheet. Renoa does not send anything on its own.

Purchases

Renoa Plus subscriptions and the Renovation Pass are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Renoa receives only a purchase confirmation and your entitlement; it never sees your payment details. Manage or cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

No cloud backup yet

This version has no cloud backup or sync. If cloud backup is added later, it will be opt-in and this policy will be updated first.

No ads, no sale of data

Renoa shows no advertising and never sells household or project data.

Children

Renoa is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Because all data lives on your device, you can delete any record inside the app, delete a whole project from Project settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

Changes

We will update this page before material changes take effect. Questions: faiza.zulfiqar71@gmail.com.
